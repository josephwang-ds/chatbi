Business context:
This dataset is for an e-commerce revenue quality analysis. The goal is to understand whether revenue growth is healthy after refunds and whether high-risk customer segments require follow-up.

Tables:
- sample1 contains order-level transactions.
- sample2 contains customer profile and customer health data.

Join rules:
- sample1.customer_id = sample2.customer_id

Metric definitions:
- GMV = SUM(revenue)
- Net revenue = SUM(revenue - refund_amount)
- Gross profit = SUM(revenue - refund_amount - cost)
- Gross margin = gross profit / net revenue
- Refund rate = SUM(refund_amount) / SUM(revenue)
- High-risk customer = health_score < 50
- Active customer = is_active = 1

Recommended business questions:
- Calculate monthly GMV, net revenue, and gross margin.
- Which customer segments have the highest refund rate?
- Which channels drive the most net revenue after refunds?
- Find high-risk customers with high net revenue.
- Compare Enterprise, SMB, and Consumer gross margin.
